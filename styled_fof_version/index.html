Ocean Cleanup Crew

The bay used to be a reef. Then the tide started bringing other things in — bottles, cans, whatever the currents could carry — until the coral underneath disappeared completely.

Two crews work the water. The green squad runs vacuums and takes the plastic; the purple squad runs magnets and takes the metal. Each bird rides a buoy around the loop, clears what it can reach, and drops out when it runs dry.

Clear the surface and the reef comes back into view. That's the whole job.
